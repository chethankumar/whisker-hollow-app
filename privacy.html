Privacy Policy

Whisker Hollow for iPhone · Effective 6 October 2026

Whisker Hollow is a puzzle game that runs entirely on your iPhone. The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your game progress: levels solved, stars, yarn balls, daily streak and settings.

This is stored only in the app's storage on your device and is never sent to the developer or any third party. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple which purchases you own so it can unlock them.

Notifications

If you tap "Remind me", the app asks permission to show a local notification when your next plot is ready. These reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Whisker Hollow is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
