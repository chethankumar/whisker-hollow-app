How to play

Place one villager in every row, every column and every fenced plot. Villagers never touch, not even corner to corner. Tap a square to cross it out, double-tap to place a villager, and drag to cross out many at once. Buildings you put up become clues on later boards: tap any clue to see what it counts.

Common questions

Why do levels 61–100 open one every 12 hours?
Levels 1–60 are free to play straight through. Levels 61–100 are also free, with a new plot opening 12 hours after you build the previous one. Tap "Remind me" to get a notification. The Swift Caravan or the Village Pass removes the wait.

What does the Village Pass include?
The last two lands, Emberpeak and Starfall Isle (levels 101–120), and no waiting on levels 61–100. It's a single purchase, not a subscription.

I bought something on another device.
Open Settings or the Shop and tap "Restore purchases".

What are yarn balls for?
A yarn ball reveals one correct square, or gives you an extra heart. You earn them by settling a land and solving the daily puzzle, or you can buy packs in the Shop.

Is my progress backed up?
Progress is stored on your iPhone and included in your device backups. Purchases can always be restored from the App Store.
